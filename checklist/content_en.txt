[overview.title]
Overview

[overview.text1]
CheckList is a versatile app for creating all kinds of lists. Plan your shopping, pack for trips, organize household chores, work tasks, ideas, celebrations, recipes, pantry supplies, renovations, moving, and much more. Create your own lists from scratch or use the ready-made online catalog with 1000+ items, conveniently organized into categories.

Add items in seconds by typing just the first few letters, rearrange them with simple drag and drop, set reminders, and mark items as completed with a double tap. Keep everything important in one place and reuse lists from your history to save time on recurring tasks. Build your own catalog, add items one by one or all at once, organize them into categories, and label them with colors. Customize the app to match your needs and preferences by choosing interface colors, icons, and many other settings.

Simplicity and ease of use are at the core of CheckList’s design. Almost every action can be performed with simple gestures, taps, and swipes, without complicated menus or unnecessary confirmations. Every feature is designed to make creating, editing, and managing lists fast, intuitive, and convenient for both everyday tasks and long-term planning.
